# Manufacturing Quality Autonomy Playbook
# 制造业品质自治 Playbook

**Never Trust Human Nature — Trust the System**
**不要相信人性，只要相信体系/制度**

> By **Terence Gan** · a quality and operations leader with 20+ years in manufacturing
> 作者：**颜士尊 / Terence Gan** · 从业二十余年的品质与运营管理者

A battle-tested, de-identified handbook on quality management — written by a practitioner who got burned, not a theorist who read about it. No real data, no real customers, no real amounts — only honest methodology.

这是一本「带伤疤的生产品」——不是实验室打磨出来的完美展示品，而是真跑过生产、摔过跟头、敢把没擦干净的地方亮给你看的过来人手记。零真实品质数据、零真实客户、零真实金额，只讲方法论。
